# Evidence-to-claims matrix

Classifications concern the narrowly stated claim. Rejected extrapolations are explicit review stress tests, not assertions that the existing reports made those claims. SUPPORTED does not mean generality or statistical significance; QUALIFIED requires the stated scope; INCONCLUSIVE leaves the comparison unresolved; UNSUPPORTED must not be promoted.
